Wind sway chars:
  Still:      )|(
  Left:       (|(
  Right:      )|)
  Far left:   ((  or /|
  Far right:  ))  or |\

Amplitude by depth:
  Trunk (depth=0): no sway
  Inner branches: ±1 char
  Outer branches: ±2-3 chars
  Willow tendrils: ±3-5 chars

── PYBONSAI / CBONSAI GENERATION NOTES ───────────────────────

Branch chars by direction:
  Horizontal: "/~"
  Left lean:  "\\|"
  Vertical:   "/|\\"
  Right lean: "|/"

Leaf chars (configurable): & % # @
Growth: recursive fractal with life counter
  - Normal distribution branching (mu=2, sigma=0.5)
  - Start length: 15, leaf length: 4, layers: 8
  - Angle: ~40 degrees
  - Probabilistic direction changes via dice rolls (0-9)

For LateLetter: can pre-generate trees using seed for
deterministic garden layout (per garden_seed in .lateletter bundle)
