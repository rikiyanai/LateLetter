── RAIN (Spring/Autumn) ──────────────────────────────────────
   Per spec: Dense | streaks falling, ground row shows splashes
   Reference: anim_J_bounce.py uses \ for angled, | for vertical
   Timing: 40ms frame rate (from reference)

Rain characters by angle:
  |   — vertical (near-zero vx)
  \   — angled right (positive vx > 0.18)
  /   — angled left (negative vx, rare)

Ground splash characters (from reference):
  '   — fresh splash (life > 4)
  .   — fading splash (life <= 4)

Fragment chars (bouncing off objects):
  * (bold)  — fresh fragment (brightness > 0.65)
  . (bold)  — medium fragment (brightness > 0.35)
  ·         — fading fragment (brightness > 0.14)

Rain density by intensity:
  Light drizzle:  MAX_DROPS = 15, VY = (0.20, 0.80)
  Normal rain:    MAX_DROPS = 35, VY = (0.35, 1.20)
  Heavy rain:     MAX_DROPS = 55, VY = (0.35, 1.80)  [reference default]
  Downpour:       MAX_DROPS = 80, VY = (0.50, 2.20)

── LIGHTNING (Storm events) ──────────────────────────────────
   Per reference: Jagged bolt from sky to ground/shape
   Segment chars age: bold # → bold + → *
   Screen inverts for 2 frames (DECSCNM reverse video: \033[?5h)
   Thunder bell (\a) after distance-proportional delay

Bolt generation (from reference):
  - Random column in center 75% of screen
  - Walk downward: r += 1, c += random(-2..2) weighted center
  - Up to 2 forks branch off after row > height/5
  - Fork length: 3 to (ground - fork_row) / 2

── SNOW (Winter) ─────────────────────────────────────────────
   Per spec: Sparse · or * drifting; accumulates on plant tops

Snow particle characters:
  ·    — small flake (most common)
  *    — medium flake
  ❄    — large flake (rare, if unicode supported)
  .    — tiny flake

Snow physics (contrast with rain):
  - Much slower: VY = (0.05, 0.25)
  - Horizontal drift: VX oscillates (sine wave) for floating effect
  - No gravity acceleration (terminal velocity reached)
  - MAX_DROPS = 20 (sparse)
  - No splash on ground; instead, accumulates

Accumulation layer:
  Before snow:    After snow:
  .-'-.           .·'·-.
 ( @ @ )         ·( @ @ )·
  `-.-'           `-.-'
    |               |
   /|\             /|\
  _____           ___·__
                  ·····

    *              *        *           *
       ·            ·     ·          ·
  ·            ·          ·             ·

Snow on ground (accumulation chars):
  _ → _· → ·_ → ·· → ·*· → **· → ***

── FALLING LEAVES (Autumn) ───────────────────────────────────
   Per spec: * or , drift diagonally downward
   Inspired by rovira_isabela_leaffalling.gif (dark tree + falling leaves)

Leaf characters (by type/size):
  ,    — tiny leaf, most common
  '    — tiny leaf, alternate
  *    — medium leaf
  ~    — curled leaf
  }    — small leaf shape
  >    — falling leaf, angled
  %    — cluster

Leaf physics:
  - VY = (0.10, 0.40) — slower than rain
  - VX = oscillating sine wave + random gusts
  - Tumble: character changes each frame (rotation illusion)
    , → ' → , → ' → ...
    * → ~ → * → ~ → ...
  - No splash; leaf settles on ground row

Leaf tumble sequence (single leaf across frames):
  Frame 1:  ,
  Frame 2:   '
  Frame 3:    ,
  Frame 4:      '
  Frame 5:       ,
  Frame 6:        '  (lands on ground)

── WIND GUST EFFECT ──────────────────────────────────────────
   Not in spec but implied by wind sway (§7.1)
   Affects rain angle, leaf drift, snow drift, plant sway simultaneously

Wind indicator (optional status bar or subtle):
  Calm:     ~~~
  Breeze:   ~~>
  Wind:     ~>>
  Gust:     >>>

Wind modifies all particle VX by a gust factor:
  gust = sin(time * 0.3) * wind_strength + random_noise


── SEASONAL COMBINATIONS (per SPEC §7.3) ────────────────────

Winter (Dec-Feb):
  Active: Snow, bare trees
  Palette: Muted, conifers, dead oak
  Weather: Snow particles + occasional wind gusts

Spring (Mar-May):
  Active: Butterflies, light rain
  Palette: Flowers dominant, bright greens
  Weather: Light drizzle + occasional sun breaks

Summer (Jun-Aug):
  Active: Fireflies at dusk, birds
  Palette: Full palette, lush
  Weather: Warm breeze (plants sway gently), occasional thunderstorm

Autumn (Sep-Nov):
  Active: Falling leaves, rain
  Palette: Warm colors (yellows, reds), dead trees
  Weather: Rain + falling leaves + wind gusts
