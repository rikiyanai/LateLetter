Relevant findings
-----------------

Creatures implemented virtual pets as autonomous software agents. Its internal
architecture combined sensory-motor neural networks, behavior selection,
artificial metabolism/hormones, lifetime learning, development and genetic
variation. The system demonstrates that artificial-life depth can produce
emergent individuality rather than a fixed animation sequence.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Use the design goal—autonomy shaped by persistent internal state—but not the
full biological simulation. A deterministic state machine, legal-action filter,
utility scorer, hysteresis, personality and bounded episodic memory are more
auditable and proportionate for LateLetter.

Limit
-----

The paper describes a substantially larger artificial-life experiment. It
does not establish that neural networks or simulated biochemistry are necessary
for a convincing LateLetter animal.
