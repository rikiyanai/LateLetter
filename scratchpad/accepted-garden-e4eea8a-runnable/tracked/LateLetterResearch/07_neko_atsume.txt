Relevant findings
-----------------

Neko Atsume centers observation, a Catbook and album, and an environment that
attracts autonomous visitors. Players set invitations such as food and goods,
then discover which cats came and what they preferred. The cats are visitors
with their own timing rather than dependants continuously demanding upkeep.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Animals should initiate visits and choose among plant/fixture affordances.
Observation, discovered routines, a journal, favorite places, photographs or
mementos can develop a relationship without exposing a decaying maintenance
meter. Bundle recipients interact with the composed environment; they do not
receive placement controls.

Limit
-----

Neko Atsume includes shop and collection systems. LateLetter adopts indirect
invitation and autonomous visitation, not its currency or catalog economy.
