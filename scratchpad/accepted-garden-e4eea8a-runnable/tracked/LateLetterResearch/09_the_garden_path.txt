Relevant findings
-----------------

The game is designed for short visits, mixes real-world and game time, changes
while the player is away, and offers travelers, events, gardening and quiet
observation. The listing frames the garden as a place that can be revisited
when desired rather than a schedule that must be obeyed.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Support glance, tend and dwell sessions. Offline reconciliation should surface
at most a few notable changes and allow immediate interaction. Returning
reveals life that continued; it never opens with a debt or repair checklist.

Limit

The Nintendo page reproduces the publisher's description. It establishes
