Relevant findings
-----------------

Peridot gives each creature a distinct blend of traits, progresses it through
developmental stages, and awards growth or bond experience for desires and
interaction. Explicit bond levels make progress legible. The documented bond
level also decreases over time.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Keep distinct seeded traits, developmental stages and behavior unlocked by
relationship growth. Reject time-based bond decrease, daily-login pressure and
task-list desires. LateLetter communicates relationship primarily through
behavior and retains progress indefinitely.

Limit
-----

Peridot is an AR collection and breeding game with economy and location
systems. Those systems are outside the LateLetter transfer.
