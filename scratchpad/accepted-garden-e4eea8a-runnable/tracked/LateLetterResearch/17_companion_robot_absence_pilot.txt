Source: Long-term effect of the absence of a companion robot on older adults:
a preliminary pilot study
Publisher: Frontiers in Computer Science, 2023
URL: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computer-science/articles/10.3389/fcomp.2023.1129506/full
Accessed: 2026-07-30
Type: Peer-reviewed preliminary pilot study

Relevant findings
-----------------

The study investigated attachment during in-home companion-robot use and the
possible emotional effect of later removing the robot. It reports a small,
specific older-adult context and explicitly treats distress after removal as a
design and study concern.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Do not manufacture dependence through guilt, threats of departure, sickness,
death, expiring affection, or claims that the animal suffers when the recipient
is absent. Preserve local durable state and design explicit lifecycle/export
behavior so a relationship is not casually erased by software changes.

Limit
-----

This is preliminary and population-specific. It illustrates a plausible ethical
risk; it does not establish that LateLetter users would experience the same
effect.
