Relevant findings
-----------------

Viridi limits the simulation to a small pot of succulents growing in real time.
It supports very short check-ins as well as passive companionship while another
task is underway. Plants can be named, receive focused care, and eventually
flower. The game is forgiving but still allows death from watering errors.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Use slow persistent growth, small care rituals, named individual plants, and
visible delayed flowering. Do not import plant death or a narrow correct-
watering schedule; LateLetter plants recover and cannot be lost through absence.
