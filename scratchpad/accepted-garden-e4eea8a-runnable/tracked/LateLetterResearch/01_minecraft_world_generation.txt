Relevant findings
-----------------

Minecraft builds a world in ordered passes rather than scattering every kind
of content in one operation. Terrain precedes biomes; structures and natural
features are added later; entities arrive after the world substrate exists.
Features such as trees and flowers are governed by biome and distribution
rules. The seed supplies deterministic variation to algorithms with declared
responsibilities.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Generate room/terrain regions, fixtures and connected paths, plants, animal
anchors, collectibles, and authored additions in stable passes. A later pass
may depend on earlier successful placement. The seed selects among candidates
that already satisfy the pass's legal rules.

Limit
-----

Minecraft is a chunked voxel world. LateLetter adopts the ordering and rule
separation, not Minecraft's scale, noise fields, or block representation.
