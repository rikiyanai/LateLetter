Relevant findings
-----------------

Classic Tamagotchi makes care legible through health, hunger, happiness,
discipline, attention calls, and care mistakes. Repeated maintenance and
correct responses influence development and survival. This creates clear
responsibility and strong cause-and-effect, but also makes absence consequential.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Keep readable cause-and-effect and development changed by interaction. Reject
attention alarms, care mistakes, death, illness, bond loss, punitive need
decay, and guilt for time away. "Tamagotchi-like" is therefore not the correct
LateLetter product description.

Limit
-----

This source documents mechanics; it does not establish psychological outcomes
or prove that every player experiences attachment.
