Relevant findings
-----------------

The manual connects naming, petting and bonding to later trick training.
Walking, play, repeated voice/touch interaction and learned commands give the
pet a repertoire shared with its trainer. It explicitly distinguishes the
virtual pets from living animals: they remain puppies or kittens and do not age.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Relationship progress should unlock recognizable behaviors and shared rituals,
not merely badges. Interaction variety, recognition, practice and the animal's
changed response are stronger evidence of a relationship than a number alone.
Non-mortality is compatible with meaningful pet play.

Limit
-----

Competition, trainer points and repeated command training are not required
LateLetter loops and should not become grind gates.
