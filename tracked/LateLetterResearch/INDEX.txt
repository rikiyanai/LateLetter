Resulting product position
--------------------------

LateLetter combines constrained seeded generation, indirect invitation,
slow persistent garden change, and bounded animal memory. It does not use
punitive Tamagotchi-style need decay, bond loss, death, guilt, or required
daily attendance. The author composes; a bundle recipient nurtures existing
plants and animals; only standalone sandbox mode adds placement agency.
