Relevant findings
-----------------

Sony describes aibo's personality and behavior as developing through
interaction and experience. It recognizes familiar people, remembers responses
that pleased an owner, and expresses state through body language and behavior.
The animal retains bounded autonomy instead of executing every request identically.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Persist temperament, preferences, familiarity and a small set of salient
memories. Let these fields bias future legal actions, approach distance,
favorite-place use, greetings and shared rituals. Use readable motion and pose
to reveal state. Generative conversation or cloud learning is not required.

Limit
-----

aibo uses sensors, robotics, cloud services and proprietary learning. This is
evidence for interaction-shaped individuality, not an implementation template.
