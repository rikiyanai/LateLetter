Relevant findings
-----------------

Terraria exposes world creation as a long, ordered generation pipeline.
Several steps attempt a target number of placements but may produce fewer when
candidate positions fail their rules. Special seeds alter selected passes and
world traits rather than suspending all placement constraints.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Bound every candidate set and permit a stable omission when no legal position
exists. Required room content must block acceptance or author export; optional
content may be absent with a recorded rejection trace. Never retry forever and
never force an object into an illegal position to satisfy a requested count.

Limit
-----

The wiki documents observed Terraria behavior, not a reusable API. Exact pass
names and counts are not LateLetter requirements.
