Relevant findings
-----------------

Minecraft's tree feature treats a tree as an anchored column/trunk plus a
canopy. The schema varies trunk height and canopy offset while retaining that
structural identity. Specialized forms expose branches, trunk lean, roots,
canopy families, permitted base blocks, replacement rules, and material that
growth may pass through.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

A LateLetter tree must first satisfy trunk/root/support invariants. Its seed
then chooses height, attachment tiers, lean, branch counts, crown dimensions,
and controlled asymmetry within species ranges. Random ancestry among arbitrary
earlier organs is not an acceptable tree grammar.

Limit
-----

This reference is for Minecraft Bedrock features and includes experimental
schema material. It is evidence for constrained structure, not a compatibility
target.
