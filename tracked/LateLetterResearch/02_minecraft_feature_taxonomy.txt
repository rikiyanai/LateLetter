Relevant findings
-----------------

The documentation separates natural features, block placement, conditional
placement, and grouping. Conditional placement searches for suitable surfaces
and controls density. Sequential groups make a later feature depend on the
successful position of an earlier feature, whereas aggregate groups evaluate
their children independently. Examples distinguish plants that require water
from plants that merely require soil-like ground.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Every candidate needs explicit surface, adjacency, dependency, clearance, and
exclusion rules. Pond, bridge, and water-lily composition should be a sequence:
failure to place the pond blocks its dependants and produces a diagnostic.
Independent decorative alternatives should use aggregate semantics.
