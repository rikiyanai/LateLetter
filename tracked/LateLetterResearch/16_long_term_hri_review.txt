Source: Long-Term Interactions with Social Robots: Trends, Insights, and Recommendations
Authors: Kayla Matheus, Rebecca Ramnauth, Brian Scassellati, Nicole Salomons
Journal: ACM Transactions on Human-Robot Interaction 14(3), 2025
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/3729539
Accessed: 2026-07-30
Type: Peer-reviewed review article

Relevant findings
-----------------

The review analyzes 120 long-term human-robot interaction studies conducted
between 2003 and 2023 across several domains. It treats repeated interaction
with the same people over multiple sessions and days as a separate evaluation
problem involving autonomy, modality, deployment context and sustained use.

LateLetter transfer
-------------------

Animal acceptance cannot be established by reducer tests or a single scripted
scene. Evaluation must cover repeated sessions, return after absence,
recognizable preference/memory changes, novelty loss, and whether the animal's
behavior remains legible and welcome over time.

Limit
-----

The review spans heterogeneous physical robots and domains. It supports a
longitudinal evaluation requirement, not a specific LateLetter effect claim.
