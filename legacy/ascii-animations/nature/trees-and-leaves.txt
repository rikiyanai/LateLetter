── FALLING LEAF PATTERNS (from GIF inspiration) ──────────────
   The rovira_isabela_leaffalling.gif shows dark gnarled tree
   with delicate leaf particles drifting down

Leaf types for different trees:

  Oak leaf:     , ' (simple comma/apostrophe)
  Maple leaf:   * ~ (star for shape, tilde for curled)
  Willow leaf:  . · (tiny, delicate)
  Pine needle:  - \ / (straight, directional)

Leaf-on-tree to leaf-falling transition:

  Before (tree with leaves):     After (leaves falling):
        .~~~.                         .~~~.
       / . . \                       /     \        ,
      | . . . |                     |       |     '
       \ . . /                       \     /   *
        '---'                         '---'       ~
          |                             |       ,
          |                             |     '
        __|__                         __|__
                                              ,   '
                                                *
