── AMBIENT BIRD (§7.2) ───────────────────────────────────────
   "Flies across sky in V or > formation"
   Separate from letter-bird; simple silhouette

Single bird flight (4 frames, loop):

Frame 1:    Frame 2:    Frame 3:    Frame 4:
  \   /       _   _       .   .       \   /
   \ /         \ /         \_/         >->
    V           v           v


Minimal bird (2-char, for distant/small):

Frame 1:  Frame 2:  Frame 3:
  v         ~         ^

Even simpler (1 char, for V-formation):
  v    ^    ~    `


Bird flock / V-formation (moves left-to-right across sky):

Frame 1:                Frame 2:               Frame 3:
            v                     ~                       ^
          v   v                 ~   ~                   ^   ^
        v       v             ~       ~               ^       ^


Bird perching (on tree branch):

  Perched:        Taking off:       In flight:
   (o>             (o>                \(o>/
   /|               /\>                 |
   / \             /  \

── FIREFLY / LIGHTNING BUG (§7.2) ────────────────────────────
   "Small * blinks on/off in lower third, only in night mode"
   Summer dusk/night animation

Firefly blink cycle (8 frames):

Frame 1-2:  *  (bright, visible)
Frame 3:    .  (dimming)
Frame 4-6:     (dark, invisible)
Frame 7:    .  (warming up)
Frame 8:    *  (bright again)

Multiple fireflies (lower third of screen, random blink offsets):

Frame 1:     ....*........*...........*....
Frame 2:     ....*.........*...........*...
Frame 3:     ...............*..........*...
Frame 4:     ......*.....................*..
Frame 5:     ......*..........*............
Frame 6:     .......*.........*............

Firefly characters by brightness:
  *  (bold) — full glow
  *         — normal glow
  ·         — dim
  .         — barely visible
  (space)   — dark

── SNAIL (bonus ground creature) ─────────────────────────────
   Hand-typed keyframes + inbetweens (Stone Story method)
   Crawl cycle: reach -> pull shell -> reach -> bob
   Implemented in anim_snail.py (mirrored frames for L/R)

Snail crawl loop (4 frames, facing right):

Frame 1 (reach):  Frame 2 (pull):   Frame 3 (reach):  Frame 4 (bob):
 @    @            @  @              @    @            @  @
  \  /              ||                \  /              \/
 _/"--"\_          _/"--"\_          _/"--"\_          _/"--"\_
/________\        /_______\         /________\        /________\

Movement: one character of forward progress per 8 frames.
Turnaround: mirror all frames horizontally (swap / and \, reverse lines).
