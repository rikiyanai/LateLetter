── SPECIES-BASED FLASH PATTERNS ──────────────────────────────

Photinus brimleyi (slow, sporadic):
  Cycle: 20 seconds
  Flashes at: 5.0s and 15.0s
  Random variation: ±3 seconds on cycle reset
  Character: good for calm, meditative garden

  Timeline (20s):
  |.....*.....|.....*.....|
  0    5     10    15    20

Photinus macdermotti (active):
  Cycle: 14.5 seconds
  Flashes at: 3.0s, 5.0s, 10.0s, 12.5s
  Random variation: ±3 seconds on cycle reset
  Character: more alive, summer evening feel

  Timeline (14.5s):
  |..*.*.....*.*.|
  0  3 5    10 12.5

Photinus carolinus (synchronous — dramatic):
  Cycle: 15 seconds
  Rapid sequential flashes at 0.5s intervals (0-6.5s)
  No randomization — all fireflies sync up
  Character: magical, coordinated display

  Timeline (15s):
  |***************.......|
  0              6.5    15


── GLOW CHARACTERS ───────────────────────────────────────────

Character progression (ramp up → peak → fade):
  (space) → . → · → * → * (bold) → * → · → . → (space)

With curses attributes:
  Off:       ' '   (no attr)
  Warming:   '.'   (curses.A_DIM)
  Dim glow:  '·'   (curses.A_NORMAL | yellow)
  Bright:    '*'   (curses.A_BOLD | yellow)
  Flash:     'o'   (curses.A_BOLD | bright_yellow)  [rare peak]


── PLACEMENT RULES ───────────────────────────────────────────

  - Lower 60% of screen only (ground level, near plants)
  - Cluster near garden elements (flowers, bushes, fences)
  - Active only during "night" phase of day/night cycle
  - 10-30 fireflies depending on screen size
  - Slight random drift: ±1 char every 5-10 frames
  - Never overlap with rain/snow particles
  - Brimleyi pattern for calm scenes, carolinus for special moments
